A conservator’s workflow

Useful restraint, measured in three passes

Generic enhancers hide their choices. Choir Cleanup ties each adjustment to a passage, preserves the source, and writes down exactly what changed.

  1. 01

    Place the sources

    Open the recording and a MusicXML or PDF score map. MusicXML rehearsal marks become editable timing suggestions.

    Seal the pack

    Confirm rights, then export labeled WAVs, a machine-readable manifest, and a plain-language edit receipt.

A clear boundary

No invented voices.
No black-box “restoration.”

This app uses ordinary, inspectable audio filters. They cover rumble, presence, dynamics, hum, and hiss.

It does not clone singers, reconstruct lost sound, or promise SATB isolation. It makes an honest rehearsal aid from what is actually on the tape.

A clear boundary

No invented voices.
No black-box “restoration.”

This app uses ordinary, inspectable audio filters: rumble roll-off, a small presence lift, gentle dynamics, optional hum notches, and a soft hiss ceiling.

It does not clone singers, reconstruct lost sound, or promise SATB isolation. It makes an honest rehearsal aid from what is actually on the tape.
